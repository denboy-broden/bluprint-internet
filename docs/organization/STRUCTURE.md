## Concept Definitions

| Concept | Definition | Example |
|---------|-----------|---------|
| **Organization** | Highest level entity | Owner → Management |
| **Department** | Functional grouping | Sales Department |
| **Function** | Business activity | Lead qualification |
| **Role** | Job title/position | Sales Agent |
| **Responsibility** | What a role must do | Follow up on leads |
| **Process** | Logical sequence of steps | Customer onboarding |
| **Workflow** | Implemented process | Ticket → Diagnosis → Resolution |
